# Decision Log

## 2026-08-20 — Voice cloning

**Status:** Rejected / do not restart by default.

We evaluated Vietnamese voice-cloning product directions, including generic voice cloning, video translation/dubbing, and creator/KOL script → own-voice voiceover. None had a compelling enough wedge: voice cloning itself is commoditized, competition is strong, and the creator use case was not differentiated enough.

If this topic comes up again, remind Louis of this decision before rebuilding anything.
