What's the story behind AquaStore?
️⭐️ Featured: Global B2B marketplace for pool, spa, water treatment and irrigation industries.

AquaStore was created in response to a long-standing structural problem in the water industry: suppliers, buyers, contractors and project opportunities remain fragmented across countries, personal networks, trade exhibitions and disconnected digital channels. Founder Aydin Malikov developed the concept after years of direct experience in pool construction, distribution, international sourcing and cooperation with manufacturers across multiple markets. AquaStore was established to transform that industry knowledge into a scalable global platform that makes supplier discovery, procurement and cross-border B2B trade more efficient, transparent and accessible.

How would you describe the primary audience of Blastra?
️⭐️ Featured: SaaS Listings Management Platform that Actually Does the Work

Blastra serves B2B software companies that need to manage product narrative across third-party platforms. Common users include small teams shipping frequent product updates who struggle to keep the world informed, large companies with multiple existing listings but no system for keeping them centralized and current, companies coming out of stealth establishing third-party presence for the first time, post-funding companies building credibility with enterprise buyers through reviews and badges, and companies going through a rebrand or pivot needing consistent updates across every platform.

What makes Orka unique?
AI-powered self-service analytics platform that lets you chat with multiple databases and get instant insights, charts, and reports without SQL.

Orka brings all your databases into a single AI-powered conversation. Unlike traditional BI tools that require dashboards, SQL, or separate analytics workflows, Orka lets users ask questions in plain English and instantly receive insights, charts, tables, and reports. Its unique cross-database intelligence enables querying and analyzing data across MongoDB, PostgreSQL, and MySQL simultaneously, eliminating data silos and reducing dependency on analysts or engineering teams.

What's the story behind Get U Hired?
A native Apple app for tech job seekers: an AI-summarized job feed, one-tap resume tailoring, a resume builder, and an application tracker in one place.

Get U Hired started from one frustration: finding a tech role means juggling a job board, a resume tool, an ATS checker, and a spreadsheet, and redoing the same work in each. We built one app that carries a job from the moment it is posted to the offer. What's the story behind Folio Student?
Format your dissertation, thesis or internship report in minutes.

Folio Student launched in May 2026 as a spin-off of Folio Studio, a formatting tool originally built for indie authors and self-published novels. Users kept asking the same question : "Can I use this for my thesis?" Folio Student is the answer. Built in France by a solo founder, the academic version focuses on what students actually need : official citation standards, auto-generated structural elements (TOC, page numbers, lists of figures), and a print-ready PDF that meets university submission requirements. The product follows GDPR by default and keeps a transparent public registry of every academic standard it supports.
